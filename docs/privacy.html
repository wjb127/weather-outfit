Weather Outfit privacy policy

Updated 5 October 2026. Publisher: WJB Apps. Contact: wjb127@naver.com.

No account is required. Your clothing rules, selected city, forecast cache and reminder choices are stored on your device. There is no background GPS tracking. Selecting a city sends rounded coordinates and your IP address to MET Norway to retrieve weather. MET Norway processes request logs under its own privacy policy.

Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions and diagnostic information to provide ads. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before eligible advertising requests. Refusing consent or unavailable advertising does not prevent outfit recommendations. Advertising privacy choices are available when required by the consent provider.

Notifications require your optional OS permission. Their contents use the downloaded forecast and show its saved timestamp. Forecasts and clothing suggestions are general comfort guidance; users can edit all temperature thresholds. Notifications may be delayed by the OS. Open regularly to refresh seven days of reminders.

Delete local settings by uninstalling the app. No local clothing diary is uploaded to the publisher. Email us for questions; do not include sensitive personal information.

Weather data: MET Norway, CC BY 4.0. The app derives outfit suggestions from temperatures and may show cached data. MET terms · Google privacy · CC BY 4.0.
